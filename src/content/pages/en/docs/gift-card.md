## Usage

The `gift-card` component renders an interactive celestial astrolabe gift card powered by a HiDPI HTML5 `<canvas>` inside the `<celestial-gift-card>` Lit Web Component. It supports 3D spring-damped pointer tilt, specular gold foil sheen, offscreen `IntersectionObserver` pausing, and `prefers-reduced-motion`.
