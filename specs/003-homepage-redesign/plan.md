## Summary

Redesign the primary messaging and metadata of the homepage (`/es/` and `/en/`) to clearly position Glenda Ferreira's online astrology consultation service for users and search engines. This includes updating page `<title>`, `<meta name="description">`, primary `<h1>` heading, hero section text formatting, and fixing a duplicate `<h2>` bug in `ImageOverlay.astro`.
